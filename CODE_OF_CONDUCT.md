# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming, respectful language.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what benefits the community and the project.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's hardware, network, logs,
  contact details, images, or other personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insulting comments, personal attacks, publishing private
information, and other conduct that a reasonable person would consider
unprofessional are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not
include sensitive information in public issues. For security-sensitive reports,
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, reject, or revert contributions and comments that
do not align with this code of conduct.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
